Prediction Score The candidate's Prediction Score represents their overall simulation score. It reflects the candidate's full analytical performance across all tasks in the simulation.
Completed Scenarios The Completed Scenario section shows the candidate's level of engagement and indicates how many tasks they completed during the simulation.
Duration The Duration section shows the total time the candidate spent completing the simulation and compares it to the typical time range of other candidates.
Strengths The Strengths section highlights the candidate's key skills and strong areas. Together with the core-task scores, it gives a more comprehensive understanding of the candidate. The downward arrow is clickable and reveals additional details.
Required Improvements The Required Improvements section specifies the areas where the candidate struggled. Together with the core-task scores, it gives a more comprehensive understanding of the candidate. The downward arrow is clickable and reveals additional details.
Core Task Performance This section visualizes the score the candidate received for each core task. You can also view the explanation for each core task. The white line represents the goal standard—the expected performance level for each task.
Service Orientation Analysis The Service Orientation analysis provides deeper insights into the candidate's communication quality. It evaluates aspects such as speech clarity, harmony, and energy.
Physical Demands This section displays specific questions that assess the candidate's preferences and comfort level with physical job requirements (for example: lifting capacity, working outdoors in various weather conditions).
